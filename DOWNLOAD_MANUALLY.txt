VADER validation data could not be downloaded automatically.

Manual steps:
1. Go to: https://github.com/cjhutto/vaderSentiment/tree/master/additional_resources/Assorted_Datasets
2. Download these 4 files into this folder:
     tweets_GroundTruth.txt
     nyt_editorial_snippets_GroundTruth.txt
     amazon_cells_labelled_GroundTruth.txt
     movieReviewSnippets_GroundTruth.txt
3. Re-run this script to generate vader_experiment.csv

Format: score<TAB>text  (score: -4 to +4)
Label thresholds (per Hutto & Gilbert 2014):
  score >= 0.05  → positive
  score <= -0.05  → negative
  else                   → neutral
